Questions, answered.

Do I need a MIDI keyboard?

A MIDI keyboard gives the best experience. Without one, you can play notes with your computer keyboard.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11. A Mac version is not available yet.

How many computers can I use it on?

One computer per license. If you change computers, deactivate JasChord on the old one from the License window, then activate it on the new one.

Does it work offline?

Yes. JasChord needs to go online from time to time to confirm your subscription, and keeps working offline for up to 14 days in between.

How do I cancel?

Open Manage membership in your receipt email (or whop.com/orders) and cancel the subscription there. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

What if I’m not happy?

You can try JasChord free for 7 days. Cancel before the trial ends and you will not be charged.

Why does Windows warn me when I install?

JasChord is a new independent app and the installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows shows a generic warning. Click More info, then Run anyway.
